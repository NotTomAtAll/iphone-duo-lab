## Migration checklist from a custom tab bar

1. **Pick the gate.** iOS 26+ gets native `TabView` with `Tab` and glass modifiers. iOS 18–25 gets native `TabView` with `Tab` and no glass modifiers. iOS 16–17 keeps the existing custom bar, or uses `TabView` + `.tabItem` (deprecated 27.2, still works). Wrap each 26/27 modifier in an `if #available` view extension.
2. **Model tabs as an enum** and drive the view with `TabView(selection:)` and `Tab(..., value:)`.
3. **Each tab owns its own `NavigationStack`** with its own path. You need the path to pop to root on re-tap. This is common practice; Apple's samples nest a stack per tab [S].
4. **Re-tap:** wrap `selection` in a proxy `Binding`. If `newValue == selection`, pop that tab's path, or scroll to top when the path is already empty.
5. **Badges:** use `Tab.badge(Int)` or `Tab.badge(Text)`. Drop custom badge views.
6. **Icons:** prefer SF Symbols. Turn custom PNGs into template assets and check them on light, dark and Reduce Transparency.
7. **Remove all bar background, colour and appearance customization.** Use a single tint at most.
8. **Conditional 5th tab:** use `.hidden(!isEligible)` rather than `if`, and reset `selection` when it disappears. Remember the HIG warns against hiding tabs.
9. **Floating PIP/mini element:** if it applies to every tab, use `tabViewBottomAccessory` (26+; `isEnabled:` is 26.1) and adapt to `.inline`. Free-floating or draggable UI stays as an overlay and must read safe-area and reserved regions itself.
10. **Hide on detail/full-screen:** put `.toolbarVisibility(.hidden, for: .tabBar)` on the pushed view. On 16–17 use `.toolbar(.hidden, for: .tabBar)`. Test pop and swipe-back on 26.x.
11. **Full-bleed content (video):** extend backgrounds with `ignoresSafeArea()` and keep controls inside the safe area. Consider `.tabBarMinimizeBehavior(.onScrollDown)` only where the content scrolls.
12. **Gestures on tab items** (long-press, double-tap): there is no native equivalent. Drop them or move them into a menu in the screen's content.
13. **Foldable/iPad:** decide on `.sidebarAdaptable` + `.defaultTabBarPlacement(.sidebar)` (27+) for the regular-width inner display. Lay out from size classes, never from orientation or idiom.
14. **Ship without** `UIDesignRequiresCompatibility`, because it is ignored with the 27 SDK.

## Gotchas

- **Custom appearance fights glass.** `UITabBarAppearance` and `backgroundColor` interfere with Liquid Glass [P]. Reports also mention squashed items on rotation and ignored named colours [S, forums 822878/825708].
- **`hidesBottomBarWhenPushed` regressions on 26.x:** the bar stays up, or reappears with no animation on back. The tab bar has also been reported showing during swipe-back [S, forums 789660/802302/805740].
- **Rebuilding a `UITabBarController` while a modal is dismissing** causes rendering glitches. Swap it after the dismiss completes [S, fatbobman].
- **Minimize needs scrolling content under the bar.** It does nothing on static screens [S].
- **The bottom accessory is global**, not per tab. Use `isEnabled:` (26.1) to toggle it [P/S].
- **`.hidden` tabs keep their state**, but a selected hidden tab leaves `selection` pointing at nothing. Reset it [P].
- **Search tab:** if no tab has `.search`, `.searchable` applies to every tab and search resets on each switch [P].
- **`TabViewCustomization` needs `customizationID`** on every customizable tab and only matters for adaptable sidebar/bar on iPad [P].
- **Performance:** no Apple numbers for the tab bar specifically. The HIG/overview say not to overuse custom glass effects [P]. Otherwise unverified.

**1 of 8 responded.** It collapsed in 8 of 8, but each ignored tap seems to restart the idle timer, which is likely why the fixer saw it "never collapse". So this is the initial expanded state after any launch with a topic/option (deep link or launch args). It is **not** specific to deep links. ## Verdict

Close, but not yet. Most of the round-3 P2s are fixed and look good: grid gutter on the fold, the zoom-from-card entrance, menu pickers, honest hinge and keyboard readings, the side-by-side topics, the fold-aware Device tab, and the copy. Three things would show up within a minute of a designer picking it up. First, the lab bar is dead right after any launch into an option, which is exactly how a demo link gets shared, and every tap restarts its timer so it looks frozen. Second, the new top-trailing chip lands on real controls in several demos (Two panes' Album segment, Laptop's skip button, Media's viewer count). Third, Transitions' recommended grid puts photos on the fold. Fix those three and it is fine to show. The rest is P3 polish.

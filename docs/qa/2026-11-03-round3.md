### P2 (visibly wrong)

2. **The lab bar and picker cover the thing being demoed** (default spot bottom-leading), in at least: Tab bar › accessory (strip), Toolbars › system-bottom (status), Media › split/full-bleed (input), Overlays › all (control strip; in book the whole leading group of split-controls), Presentations › popover/menu ("Left edge" button), Laptop › overlay/controls-top/laptop-only (transport), Toasts › all ("Add a note" and status line), Fold mid-task (editor, with keyboard). The bar drags, but a first-timer won't know, and closed it is the default. Repro: launch any of these, closed pose. Screenshots in `$S/sweep/*-1closed.png`.
3. **Hinge angle goes stale after ‹ ›.** Hinge, closed: open Book pages, press ‹ seven times. Every option reads "180° · waiting for first change · fully open" while closed, until you fold. `$S/hingestale.jpg`, `$S/step-hinge.jpg`.
4. **Media › Fixed side panel, closed:** 85 pt video sliver; "1.2k watching" wraps one letter per line; LIVE badge cut; chat runs under the rail; bar's leading edge off-screen (Close hit point x 17). `$S/sweep/media-fixed-panel-1closed.png`, final screenshot of `$S/step-media.jpg`.
5. **Picker truncation** (fixed 220 pt width): Toasts 6 segments unreadable; Toolbars "System… Keep act…"; Hinge "Effect al… Column…"; Laptop "Laptop (simula…". Every pose. `$S/g10.jpg`, `$S/g2.jpg`, `$S/g7.jpg`.
6. **Open thumbnails draw rail items at the fold x.** On topic pages in Open mode, rail-placed elements (tab pill, compose/more, toolbar items, back chevron) render in the middle of the content (~475 pt), not at the trailing edge. Live open pose is correct. Tab bar / Toolbars / Hide-on-push cards. `$S/shots/crop1.png`.
7. **First live frame lays out at the wrong width, then jumps.** Opening an option shows list text wrapped narrow, buttons one letter wide (Toasts), a 1-column grid or "Container 296 pt" for a frame or two, then it reflows. On a closed device the entrance is a cross-fade, never the card zoom, because folded thumbnails aren't rendered while the switch says Open. For ~150 ms the topic page also shows under the bottom ~45 pt. `$S/trans-pair.jpg`, `$S/trans-tabbar2.jpg`, `$S/step-toasts.jpg`.
8. **Fold mid-task:** the checklist panel covers the title. With the keyboard up the bar moves onto the editor, so taps meant for the bar hit keys. The identity option's own sheet hides the bar. After a fold round trip on "above", "Keyboard focus ✓" shows with no keyboard. `$S/shots/dark-continuity.png`, `$S/sweep/continuity-above-4closed.png`.
9. **Wrong copy:** Tab bar › native "Try this" (bar never moves to the bottom). List › three-column con (book shows three). Grids › adaptive-large con (folded is 1 column). Grids › adaptive-even pro (see #1).
10. **Device tab, book:** "Show the measure overlay" and "Show thumb reach zones" toggles sit on the fold (x ≈ 455–495), and the Reach paragraph runs across it. The app's own rule broken on its own screen. `$S/dev.jpg` (2nd panel).

## What delighted
- The pose sweep holds up: 70 options × 4 poses, no crash, no blank screen, no stale layout after closed→book→open→closed.
- Form sheet in book pose lands on the left half, clear of the fold. List/detail and media splits put the divider exactly on the fold.
- The Device tab reads live and is honest: 90° / 180° / 0°, reserved regions with x/w, safe-area diagram, reach bands.
- Info sheets are well structured: verdict, Try this, Good / Watch out, Apple suggests, How it's built with the API line, plus Adjust settings and the measure toggle.
- Deep-link errors are human-readable. ‹ › wraps cleanly. The bar's drag-to-snap has a haptic.
- The Fold mid-task checklist ("Selected row / Scroll position / Typed text …") is a great idea.

## What a first-time user won't understand
- That the bar can be dragged, or that it's covering half the demo.
- "Open / Folded" on the topic page: does it change the device or the pictures? On a closed phone it defaults to Open.
- "the rail", "division", "Device Hub", "interfaceOrientation", "variant" knobs in Adjust settings.
- Why Minimize on scroll looks identical to Native (you have to scroll and know it does nothing).
- Verdict words: "Built in", "Effect", "Worth a try" vs "Recommended": no legend.
- Fold mid-task: what to do. The checklist starts empty, the keyboard pops up and the bar jumps.
- Hinge after stepping: "waiting for first change", with no hint to fold.

## 1. Bar responsiveness

### Cold launch into an option, tap ⓘ right away

| Run | Launch args | Tap at (after `simctl launch`) | Responded |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `-topic toasts -option pill` | ~2.0 s | no |
| 2 | `-topic grids -option adaptive-even` | ~2.3 s | no |
| 3 | `-topic reading -option column` | ~2.3 s | no |
| 4 | `-topic tabbar -option native` | ~2.2 s | no |
| 5 | `-topic media -option arrangement-split` | ~2.0 s | no |
| 6 | `-topic hinge -option effect-only` | ~2.4 s | **yes** |

**1 of 6.** The tool round trip means my earliest tap landed at ~2.0 s, not 1.5 s.

New finding: **the whole option screen ignores input for the first ~2.5 s, not only the bar.** Controls:
- Toasts › pill: tapping the demo's own Save button at ~2.4 s did nothing. At ~3.8 s it worked ("Saved Morning walk" pill).
- Toasts › pill: ⓘ at ~3.3 s worked (info sheet opened).
- Cold launch with no args: a home row tapped at ~2 s opened right away. So it is specific to launching straight into an option.
- The bar's idle timer is 3 s (`FloatingBar.idleDelay`). That leaves a usable window of about 0.5 s before it folds. In practice, someone who taps the bar they see at launch gets nothing, and then it folds away. Once it has folded, the handle works every time.

Improvement since round 4: the ignored taps no longer keep the bar stuck open. It always folds by ~3–4 s, and the handle then expands a bar that works. Cause not confirmed. The thumbnail windows sit below `.normal` with `isUserInteractionEnabled = false`, so they are probably not swallowing touches. The launch-time presentation is the likelier suspect.

## 4. First-time flow as a designer

Fresh state note: `xcrun simctl spawn <udid> defaults delete com.example.duolab` fails with "Domain not found". The app's prefs live in its container, and deleting the plist file still left `duolab.tourSeen` cached. I uninstalled and reinstalled the same bundle to get a true first run.

Walkthrough (closed): home → Start the tour → watched 1–4 → Close → List and detail → Open/Folded → opened the Recommended option → ⓘ → Adjust settings → changed "Folded shows" → back → Done → Close → Reading width → Folded/Open.

Moments of confusion:
1. **Home.** "Drag the floating bar out of the way" talks about a bar I haven't seen yet. Now that the bar folds into a handle by itself, the instruction is also out of date. The tiny topic thumbnails are unreadable at that size: grey lorem lines.
2. **Tour strip, closed.** The last button is labelled just "Open". The topic page's switch is also "Open / Folded", so on a folded phone I read it as "show the open-phone version", not "open this topic". The ▯▯ Compare icon has no label. A touch silently holds the tour with no feedback.
3. **Topic page.**
   - The  line is Apple's guidance, but nothing says so.
   - "Open / Folded" changes the pictures, not the device. It defaults to Open on a closed phone, and nothing explains that.
   - The question heading changes size when you flip Open/Folded (larger in Open, `$S/shots` sequence 03:21:50 vs 03:22:00), which reads as a glitch.
   - The verdict legend is only at the bottom.
4. **Option screen.**
   - The bar is titled "Two columns: list + detail", but the closed phone shows one column with a back button. You have to know to unfold.
   - The bar folds away 3 s after launch, after ⓘ's sheet closes, and after every touch. 3 s is shorter than it takes to read the title and decide. A tap where the bar used to be then hits the content: I selected a list row by accident in Motion.
   - The handle has no label or icon, only a dot and a grabber line. It reads as a scroll indicator.
5. **ⓘ sheet.** Clear structure (Recommended, Try this, Good, Watch out, Apple suggests). But "Adjust settings" and "Show measurements" are at the very bottom, under "How it's built" with its API line. A designer stops reading at the code block and never finds them.
6. **"Adjust settings" opens a page titled "Fine-tune".** The label doesn't match.
   - Copy is developer-speak: "HIG:", "Column visibility · Automatic picks per size class", "Split style: Balanced / prominent detail", "Google's rule".
   - The way back is a chevron in the rail strip, not in the sheet's header, so it looks like it belongs to the screen behind.
   - After changing a setting, the bar still says "Recommended · 1 of 8". Nothing marks the option as modified, and on a closed phone that setting changes nothing visible.
7. **Missing:**
   - No in-app hint to physically fold or unfold the phone (everything interesting about the Recommended answers shows in book/open).
   - No way to see Open and Folded side by side for one option outside the tour.
   - No "reset to Recommended" after fine-tuning.
   - No search on the 19-topic list.

## Verdict

**Ship to designers: yes, with a heads-up note.** Round 4's three blockers are fixed or much smaller:
- The bar no longer looks frozen: it folds and the handle always works.
- The handle no longer sits on top-trailing controls.
- Transitions' grid is clear of the fold.

The tour is solid: it auto-advances, holds, steps, compares in all three poses and opens a topic. No P1. What remains won't stop a designer, but tell them to wait a beat after opening a shared link, and to unfold the phone to see the point of each answer.

Three things that would most improve it next:
1. **Make the first seconds and the bar forgiving.** Fix the ~2.5 s dead input when launching into an option. Keep the bar expanded until the first interaction or ~8 s. Give the handle a recognisable glyph.
2. **Finish fold-awareness on the Recommended answers in book:** Hinge effect-only rows and slider, the detail column in Fold mid-task and Motion, the Reading column, Keyboard chat. The app's own rule is "nothing on the fold", and a designer will check exactly that.
3. **Designer-facing words and wayfinding:**
   - Rename the strip's "Open" to "Try it" (or similar).
   - Move "Adjust settings" and "Show measurements" above "How it's built", and make the page title match.
   - Rewrite the Fine-tune copy without HIG / size-class / Google terms.
   - Mark "modified" in the bar.
   - Add a one-line "fold or unfold the phone to see this" hint on option screens.
